AnmolLipi, Asees, Joy, DrChatrik, GurbaniLipi, Satluj — widely used in older documents, newspapers, and government offices. Each uses different ASCII character codes to display Gurmukhi glyphs.

Unicode (Raavi)

The standard Unicode Gurmukhi font used in modern computing, web, and mobile. Unicode range: U+0A00 – U+0A7F. Converting to Unicode ensures your text works everywhere.
